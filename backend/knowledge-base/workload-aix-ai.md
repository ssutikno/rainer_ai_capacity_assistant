# AIX AI Workload Baseline v1.3.0

| Workload | GPU awal | System memory | Dataset storage | Network |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Inference/RAG | 1 GPU, minimum 24 GB VRAM | 128 GB | 1 TB NVMe | Dual 25 GbE |
| Computer vision | 1 GPU, minimum 24 GB VRAM | 128 GB | 2 TB NVMe | Dual 25 GbE |
| Training/fine-tuning | 2 GPU, minimum 48 GB VRAM/GPU | 256 GB | 4 TB NVMe | Dual 25/100 GbE |

Tier meningkatkan jumlah GPU, RAM, storage, dan fabric. Model, precision, context length, concurrency, framework, power, cooling, GPU support matrix, dan lisensi wajib divalidasi.
